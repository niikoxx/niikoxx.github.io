Yet another Sunday morning. Maybe I should be writing more often just for myself whatever this “writing for myself” means. In the end writing for me offer two main but different values.



One of them is to create art and the other is to bring order to the storm, chaos, puzzledness of thoughts in my. I guess writing is some kind of imaginary conversation I want to pursuit right now but can for various reasons. This does not only apply to myself as a person perceiving myself as more distant, disconnected and isolated then other people. It is in human nature I believe, that we from time to time feel the desire to talk about what matters right now - what lays underneath a veil on our souls - but despite maybe being fortunate enough having a person talking to, feeling heard, seen or truly understood will never always be truly available for no one. The cause lays in human nature I believe. Oftentimes, talking to another person trustworthy and important enough to open up on, verbalising the matter deep within, sharing thoughts, impressions and associations unwinds a lot of chaos and releases weight, cleansing the veil. Pen and paper turn into a tool of imaginary and spiritual conversation all alone in company of myself.



The other one for me - as I have already mentioned - is writing as a form of art. Now of course the question “what is and what isn’t art” immediately arises to me. Without thinking about it for long for me the answer appears to be offering an inside into my life, reality, soul, perspective or whatever the appropriate word to describe this thing might be. It’s not actually about the question “what is art” I want to focus on but moreso about why I write and how I do want to approach writing for the future. Offering an inside to my life and soul still isn’t the answer to both questions just mention. As much as I would love to solve these mysteries upon those questions I will cut this interrogation (for now) for the better.



I have come to realise that the core about why and how I do write has shifted dramatically. I can’t remember for sure when and how I start writing yet I can recall it being connected with the beginning of my interest for books. I have started reading out of curiosity and the realisation that I know quite little. Improving myself especially my well-being has to be at the beginning of my love towards writing. First as a method to study topics and information and store them in some material way in form of writing, then increasingly to study and reflect on my existence and with time also on the world myself wonders around in. I have come to realise that art is a way for many of us to express their way of wondering around as a person in this world leaving us all with questions.



Writing seems to be my tool to express, my way of creating. As I’ve mentioned and described: why I write has changed dramatically over the years. This shift of purpose has taken place quietly in the background. Over the course of this process, my style of writing has increasingly changed as well just without me being aware about it. The “Why” about my relation to writing has impacted the “how” immensely and simultaneously in total silence and without my consent. While writing started out as a matter of functional reasons it has become a matter of “how will my written texts be perceived by the readers”. Writing has become a tool to be seen, understood and visible and not an activity done by and for myself. It has increasingly become more frustrating and a source of stress instead of a source of joy and about brutal honesty towards myself which it has been somewhere in the middle of my writing process. I wrote for myself knowing it won’t be read by anyone I wouldn’t want to.



Now the question always arises “is this great” or “is this finished”. My creations can’t be private anymore. They are nowadays always exposed and judged. I am creating imaginary people reading my creations, telling me what is and what isn’t good about what I have created. My purpose about writing has shifted, my goal has changed and my expectations have grown. Since I do only genuinely disagree on the risen expectations I put upon myself in consequence of the different purpose and different goals, I believe that on more Sunday mornings I should be writing just for myself maybe offering an insight to my life and soul wondering around in this world.
